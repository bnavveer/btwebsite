Our home yard

Bay Transport is a family-owned, asset-based trucking company headquartered in Union City. Our trucks, trailers and gated, monitored yard are here on Zwissig Way, so Bay Area shippers deal directly with the company that moves their freight.

What we run out of Union City

  • Full truckload and less than truckload shipping, local and long-haul
  • Dedicated drop trailers at Bay Area facilities
  • Secure cargo storage in sealed trailers at our yard
  • Local runs across the Bay Area and Northern California
  • Long-haul freight to all 48 contiguous states

We're open daily from 7 am to 5 pm Pacific. Stop by the yard, call the office, or message us on WhatsApp.
