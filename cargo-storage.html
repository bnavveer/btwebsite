Secure trailer and cargo storage

Load a trailer, seal it, and we hold it at our gated, monitored yard until you need it. A lower-cost alternative to warehouse space.

What it is

Our storage program lets you skip costly warehousing. Your freight is loaded into one of our trailers and sealed, and the sealed trailer is held at one of our secure facilities. When you're ready, we deliver it, so storage and transportation are handled by one company.

When it's the right fit

  • You need overflow space for seasonal inventory
  • You're between warehouse leases or moving facilities
  • Freight needs to wait before its final delivery
  • You'd rather not pay for warehouse space short-term

Common questions

Where is the freight stored?

In our gated, monitored yards. Our main yard is at 34203 Zwissig Way in Union City, and we have a second facility in Stockton.

How long can you store a trailer?

Contact dispatch with your timeline. We've stored 30 truckloads of medical supplies for one customer over several years.

Is storage cheaper than a warehouse?

For many shippers, yes. A sealed trailer in our yard avoids warehouse handling and lease costs, and delivery is handled by the same company.
