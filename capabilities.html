Bay Transport Inc.

Capabilities statement

Company overview

Bay Transport Inc. is an asset-based transportation and logistics provider, family-owned and operated since 2005. Based in California with operations across the United States, we serve clients of every size with services tailored to their needs.

Core services

  • Freight transportation, full truckload (FTL) and less than truckload (LTL)
  • Dedicated drop trailers at customer facilities
  • Cargo storage in gated, monitored yards
  • EDI and API integration for tenders, tracking and invoices
  • Logistics management and consulting

Differentiators

  • Over 20 years of industry experience
  • Asset-based 3PL: we own the trucks, trailers and yards
  • 100% tracking on every shipment, origin to destination
  • Drop trailers reduce loading and unloading times
  • Trailers double as cost-effective extra storage

Past performance

Vander-Bend Manufacturing 960 full truckloads delivered to locations in California, Georgia and Kansas, December 2018 to September 2023. All shipments tracked with on-time pickup and delivery, no cargo damage and intact seals.
Aeronet Road Transport Storage for 30 full truckloads of medical supplies, with pickup and delivery arranged, July 2020 to March 2023.
