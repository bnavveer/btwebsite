What we haul and handle

Every shipment is tracked from pickup to delivery, whatever the size.

  • Full truckload

    A whole trailer for your freight, going straight from your dock to the receiver's. No transfers, no extra handling.

    FTL

  • Less than truckload

    For shipments that don't fill a trailer. You pay for the space your freight uses, not the whole truck.

    LTL

  • Dedicated drop trailers

    We leave an empty trailer at your facility. Your team loads it on your schedule, and we collect it when it's ready.

    At your dock

  • Secure cargo storage

  • EDI and API integration

No broker in the middle

Many trucking companies are brokers: they take your load and pass it to whoever is available. Bay Transport is asset-based, so your freight stays with us from dock to dock.

  • One company accountable

    Our own fleet, drivers and safety program. If something needs fixing, there's one number to call.

  • No middleman markup

    Without broker fees, more of your budget moves freight. Steady shippers can get volume pricing.

  • Room to grow with you

    Our equipment, so we can add trucks, drop trailers or storage quickly for peak season.

  • Partners for the long haul

    We learn how your business works and build long-term relationships, not one-off loads.
