We own the trucks. We hire and train the drivers. We run the yards in Union City and Stockton. The company you call is the company that moves your freight.

What we haul and handle

Every shipment is tracked from pickup to delivery, whatever the size.

  • Full truckload (FTL)

    A whole trailer for your freight, going straight from your dock to the receiver's. No transfers and no extra handling along the way.

  • Less than truckload (LTL)

    For shipments that don't fill a trailer. You pay for the space your freight uses, not the whole truck.

  • Dedicated drop trailers

    We leave an empty trailer at your facility. Your team loads or unloads it on your own schedule, and we collect it when it's ready.

  • Secure cargo storage

    Load a trailer, seal it, and we hold it at our gated, monitored yard until you need it. A lower-cost alternative to renting warehouse space.

  • EDI and API integration

    Send load tenders, get tracking updates and receive invoices straight into your own systems, without phone tag or spreadsheets.

  • Logistics planning

    Help planning lanes, schedules and capacity, whether you're preparing for a seasonal peak or opening a new distribution route.

Why it matters that we own the trucks

Many trucking companies are brokers. They take your load and hand it to whoever is available. We don't.

One company accountable

We maintain our own fleet, train our own drivers and run our own safety program. If something needs fixing, you have one number to call and one company responsible.

No middleman markup

Without broker fees in the price, more of your budget goes into moving freight. Steady shippers can also get volume pricing.

Room to grow with you

Because the equipment is ours, we can add trucks, drop trailers or storage space quickly when your volume jumps.

How a load moves with us

  1. Request a quote

    Tell us what you're shipping, where and when. We reply with a price and a pickup plan.

  2. Pickup

    Our driver arrives at your dock, or collects a drop trailer your team has already loaded.

  3. Tracked in transit

    Every load is tracked from origin to destination. Check in with dispatch or get updates through EDI.

  4. Delivered

    Your freight arrives at the receiving dock, sealed and accounted for.

An American family business

We're a minority-owned, Asian-Indian American owned small business, built the way American trucking has always been built: by showing up, keeping our word and getting the freight there.

Twenty years on, our trailers run coast to coast with the same promise painted on the side: dock to dock, around the clock.
