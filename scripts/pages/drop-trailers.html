What it is

With a drop trailer, there's no truck waiting at your dock while your team works. We drop a trailer at your facility, you load or unload it when it suits your shift, and we come back for it when you call. It takes the pressure off loading and unloading, and a parked trailer can double as extra storage space.

When it's the right fit

  • Your dock gets busy and live loading causes delays
  • Your team loads across several shifts or days
  • You want to avoid paying for truck wait time
  • You need temporary extra storage at your site

Common questions

What is a drop trailer?

A trailer we leave at your facility so your team can load or unload it without a truck and driver waiting. We pick it up when it's ready.

Can a drop trailer be used for storage?

Yes. A trailer parked at your site can work as extra, cost-effective storage space.

Do you offer drop trailers outside the Bay Area?

Contact dispatch with your location. We run from Union City and Stockton and serve the 48 contiguous states.
