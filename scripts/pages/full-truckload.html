What it is

With full truckload, your shipment has the trailer to itself. It moves directly from pickup to delivery without stopping at terminals or being reloaded onto other trucks. Fewer touches means less handling, fewer delays and less risk of damage.

When it's the right fit

  • Your freight fills most or all of a trailer
  • The load is time-sensitive and needs a direct run
  • The freight is fragile or high-value and should be handled as little as possible
  • You ship the same lane regularly and want one carrier you can count on

Common questions

Are you a broker or the carrier?

We're the carrier. Bay Transport is asset-based: we own the trucks and trailers and employ the drivers, so your load isn't handed to another company.

Where do you run full truckloads?

From our yards in Union City and Stockton, California to anywhere in the 48 contiguous states, plus local runs across the Bay Area and Northern California.

Can you handle a recurring lane?

Yes. Many of our customers ship the same lanes on a schedule. We delivered 960 full truckloads for one manufacturer between 2018 and 2023, all tracked and on time.
