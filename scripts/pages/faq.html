About Bay Transport

Is Bay Transport a broker or a carrier?

A carrier. Bay Transport is asset-based: we own our trucks, trailers and yards and employ our own drivers, so your load is never handed off to another company. Our MC number is 521732 and our USDOT number is 1361987.

How long have you been in business?

Since 2005. Bay Transport is family-owned and operated, headquartered in Union City, California, with a second facility in Stockton.

Are you a minority-owned business?

Yes. Bay Transport is a minority-owned, self-certified small disadvantaged business, and Asian-Indian American owned.

Where can I find your carrier credentials?

On our capabilities page: MC 521732, USDOT 1361987, SCAC BACV, CAGE 8JLA4 and UEI XPS9GHLDMDD5, with copy buttons and a link to verify us on FMCSA.

Shipping with us

What areas do you serve?

All 48 contiguous states, plus local runs across the Bay Area and Northern California, out of our yards in Union City and Stockton.

What services do you offer?

Full truckload (FTL), less than truckload (LTL), dedicated drop trailers, secure cargo storage, EDI and API integration, and logistics planning.

Do you track shipments?

Yes. Every shipment is tracked from origin to destination. You can check in with dispatch, or receive updates through EDI.

What is a drop trailer?

A trailer we leave at your facility so your team can load or unload it on your own schedule, without a truck waiting. We pick it up when it's ready.

Can you store my freight?

Yes. Your freight is loaded into a trailer and sealed, and we hold the sealed trailer in our gated, monitored yard until you need it delivered.

Can you handle seasonal peaks?

Yes. Because the equipment is ours, we can add trucks, drop trailers or storage when your volume jumps.

Can you connect to our systems?

Yes. We support EDI and API integration for load tenders, tracking updates and invoices.

Quotes and contact

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form on our website, call (510) 475-6100, message us on WhatsApp at (510) 246-5453, or email Dispatch@baytransportinc.com. Dispatch replies with a price and a pickup plan.

What information do you need for a quote?

The service you need, pickup and delivery ZIP codes, the pickup date, what the freight is, and the weight or pallet count if you have them.
