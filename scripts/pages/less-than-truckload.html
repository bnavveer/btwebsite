What it is

Less than truckload is for a few pallets or a partial load. Instead of paying for a whole trailer, you pay for the part your freight takes up. It's the economical choice when your shipment is too big for parcel but too small for a full truckload.

When it's the right fit

  • You're shipping a few pallets or a partial load
  • You want to keep costs down without giving up tracking
  • Your shipments vary in size from week to week
  • You need a pickup in the Bay Area or Central Valley

Common questions

How is LTL priced?

By the space and weight your shipment needs rather than a whole trailer. Send the details through the quote form and dispatch will reply with a price.

Is LTL freight tracked?

Yes. Every shipment we carry is tracked from origin to destination.

When should I choose full truckload instead?

When your freight fills most of a trailer, is time-sensitive, or should be handled as little as possible. If you're not sure, choose "Not sure yet" on the quote form and we'll help.
