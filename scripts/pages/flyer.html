What we do

Bay Transport Inc. is an asset-based logistics company that takes the guesswork out of shipping for shippers, manufacturers and distributors. We provide supply chain solutions and logistical services to all 48 contiguous states, with a proven record of being capable and reliable.

With 20+ years in the industry, your cargo is in experienced hands.

Who we are

We've been family-owned and operated since 2005 and have grown into one of Northern California's premier logistics providers. We build long-term partnerships with our clients and tailor solutions to their transportation and logistics challenges.

Sustainable supply chain solutions are the key to our success, and to the success of our clients.
